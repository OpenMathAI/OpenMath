## 四、复现查询（MySQL）

```sql
-- 得主两两 co-honored（两端的 people.qid 都在 161 人清单内）
SELECT pa.name_en, pb.name_en, r.note
FROM person_relation r
JOIN people pa ON pa.id = r.from_id
JOIN people pb ON pb.id = r.to_id
WHERE r.relation_type = 'co-honored'
  AND pa.qid IN (SELECT qid FROM people WHERE primary_occupation='physicist' AND has_social_data=1)
  AND pb.qid IN (SELECT qid FROM people WHERE primary_occupation='physicist' AND has_social_data=1);

-- 得主师承链（导师→学生，双方均为 161 人清单）
SELECT pa.name_en AS advisor, pb.name_en AS student
FROM person_relation r
JOIN people pa ON pa.id = r.from_id
JOIN people pb ON pb.id = r.to_id
WHERE r.relation_type = 'advisor-student';
```
