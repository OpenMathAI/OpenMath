**Robert Betts Laughlin** (born November 1, 1950) is an American physicist. He is the Anne T. and [Robert M. Bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Bass_(businessman) "Robert Bass (businessman)") Professor of [Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics "Physics") and [Applied Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Physics "Applied Physics") at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University "Stanford University"). Along with [Horst L. Störmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horst_L._Störmer "Horst L. Störmer") of [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University") and [Daniel C. Tsui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_C._Tsui "Daniel C. Tsui") of [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University "Princeton University"), he was awarded a share of the 1998 [Nobel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize "Nobel Prize") in [physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics "Physics") for their explanation of the [fractional quantum Hall effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_quantum_Hall_effect "Fractional quantum Hall effect").

In 1983, Laughlin was first to provide a [many body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Many-body_physics "Many-body physics") [wave function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_function "Wave function"), now known as the *[Laughlin wavefunction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laughlin_wavefunction "Laughlin wavefunction"),* for the [fractional quantum Hall effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_quantum_Hall_effect "Fractional quantum Hall effect"), which was able to correctly explain the fractionalized charge observed in experiments. This state has since been interpreted as the integer quantum Hall effect of the [composite fermion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Composite_fermion "Composite fermion").

His 2017 paper, "Pumped thermal grid storage with heat exchange" inspired Project Malta at [Google X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_X "Google X") and subsequently Malta inc.

## Biography

Laughlin was born in [Visalia, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visalia,_California "Visalia, California"). He earned a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts "Bachelor of Arts") in [mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics "Mathematics") at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley "University of California, Berkeley") in 1972, and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy "Doctor of Philosophy") in physics in 1979 at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology "Massachusetts Institute of Technology") (MIT). Between 2004 and 2006, he served as the president of [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST "KAIST") in [Daejeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daejeon "Daejeon"), [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea "South Korea").

The book argues for [emergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence "Emergence") as a replacement for [reductionism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductionism "Reductionism"), in addition to general commentary on hot-topic issues.
