**Klaus von Klitzing** (German: [[ˈklaʊs fɔn ˈklɪtsɪŋ]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Standard_German "Help:IPA/Standard German") [ⓘ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:De-Klaus_von_Klitzing.ogg "File:De-Klaus von Klitzing.ogg"); born 28 June 1943) is a German physicist, known for discovery of the [integer quantum Hall effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Hall_effect "Quantum Hall effect"), for which he was awarded the 1985 [Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics "Nobel Prize in Physics").

Von Klitzing was born into the old noble [Von Klitzing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Klitzing "Von Klitzing") family from the [Mittelmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mittelmark "Mittelmark") region. He and his family arrived in Lutten near [Vechta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vechta "Vechta") as [German Expellees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Expellees "German Expellees") in 1945. In 1948 they moved to [Oldenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oldenburg_(city) "Oldenburg (city)") and in 1951 to [Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essen_(Oldenburg) "Essen (Oldenburg)") in the [Cloppenburg district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloppenburg_(district) "Cloppenburg (district)").

## Education

In Essen von Klitzing went to the Protestant elementary School where he was allowed to take advanced classes targeted at older students, and from 1953 onwards he attended the [Artland-Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artland-Gymnasium "Artland-Gymnasium") in [Quakenbrück](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quakenbrück "Quakenbrück") where he earned his [Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur "Abitur") at the top of his class in 1962.

He then studied physics at the [Braunschweig University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braunschweig_University_of_Technology "Braunschweig University of Technology"), where he received his diploma in 1969. He continued his studies at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg "University of Würzburg") at the chair of [Gottfried Landwehr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Landwehr "Gottfried Landwehr"), completing his PhD thesis entitled *Galvanomagnetic Properties of Tellurium in Strong Magnetic Fields* ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language "German language"): *Galvanomagnetische Eigenschaften von Tellur in starken Magnetfeldern*) in 1972, and gaining [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation "Habilitation") in 1978.

## Research and career

During his career Klitzing has worked at the [Clarendon Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarendon_Laboratory "Clarendon Laboratory") at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford") and the Grenoble High Magnetic Field Laboratory in France (now [LNCMI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LNCMI "LNCMI")), where he continued to work until becoming a professor at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich "Technical University of Munich") in 1980. He has been a director of the [Max Planck Institute for Solid State Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_for_Solid_State_Research "Max Planck Institute for Solid State Research") in [Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart "Stuttgart") since 1985.

The [von Klitzing constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Klitzing_constant "Von Klitzing constant"), *R*K = *[h](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck_constant "Planck constant")*/*[e](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_charge "Elementary charge")*2 = 25812.80745... Ω, is named in honor of Klaus von Klitzing's discovery of the [quantum Hall effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Hall_effect "Quantum Hall effect"), and is listed in the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology "National Institute of Standards and Technology") *Reference on Constants, Units, and Uncertainty*. The inverse of the constant is equal to half the value of the [conductance quantum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conductance_quantum "Conductance quantum").

More recently, Klitzing's research focuses on the properties of low-dimensional electronic systems, typically in low temperatures and in high [magnetic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_field "Magnetic field").

Von Klitzing has won numerous awards and honours including:

- 1982 [EPS Europhysics Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPS_Europhysics_Prize "EPS Europhysics Prize")
- 1982 [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard "Hewlett-Packard") Prize
- 1985 [Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics "Nobel Prize in Physics")
