**Louis Eugène Félix Néel** (French: [[neɛl]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/French "Help:IPA/French"); 22 November 1904 – 17 November 2000) was a French [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist "Physicist") born in [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon "Lyon") who received the [Nobel Prize for Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics "Nobel Prize in Physics") in 1970 for his studies of the magnetic properties of [solids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid "Solid").

## Biography

Néel studied at the [Lycée du Parc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_du_Parc "Lycée du Parc") in Lyon and was accepted at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure "École Normale Supérieure") in [Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris "Paris"). He obtained the degree of Doctor of Science at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg "University of Strasbourg"). He was corecipient (with the [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden "Sweden") [astrophysicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysicist "Astrophysicist") [Hannes Alfvén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannes_Alfvén "Hannes Alfvén")) of the [Nobel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize "Nobel Prize") for [Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics "Physics") in 1970 for his pioneering studies of the magnetic properties of solids. His contributions to [solid state physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_state_physics "Solid state physics") have found numerous useful applications, particularly in the development of improved [computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer "Computer") memory units. About 1930 he suggested that a new form of magnetic behavior might exist; called [antiferromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiferromagnetism "Antiferromagnetism"), as opposed to [ferromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferromagnetism "Ferromagnetism"). Above a certain temperature (the [Néel temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Néel_temperature "Néel temperature") or magnetic ordering temperature, *T*N) this behaviour stops since above the Néel temperature the material undergoes a [phase transition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_transition "Phase transition") and becomes paramagnetic. Néel pointed out (1948) that materials could also exist showing [ferrimagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrimagnetism "Ferrimagnetism"). Néel has also given an explanation of the weak magnetism of certain rocks, making possible the study of the history of [Earth's magnetic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth's_magnetic_field "Earth's magnetic field").

He is the instigator of the [Polygone Scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygone_Scientifique "Polygone Scientifique") in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble "Grenoble").

The [Louis Néel Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Néel_Medal "Louis Néel Medal"), awarded annually by the [European Geophysical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Geophysical_Society "European Geophysical Society"), is named in Néel's honour.

Néel died at [Brive-la-Gaillarde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brive-la-Gaillarde "Brive-la-Gaillarde") on 17 November 2000 at the age 95, just 5 days short of his 96th birthday.

## Awards and honours

Néel received numerous awards and honours for his work including:

### Awards

- Hughes Prize of the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences "Académie des sciences") (1935)
- Félix Robin Prize of the Société française de physique (1938)
- [André Blondel Medal](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Médaille%20Blondel "fr:Médaille Blondel") (1948)
- Grand prix du conseil de l’association « Au service de la pensée française » (1949)
- [Holweck Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernand_Holweck_Medal_and_Prize "Fernand Holweck Medal and Prize") (1952)
- Elected Foreign Member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences "Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences") (1959)
- [Three Physicists Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Physicists_Prize "Three Physicists Prize") (1963)
- Gold Medal of CNRS (1965)
- Elected [Foreign Member of the Royal Society (ForMemRS) in 1966](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_1966 "List of Fellows of the Royal Society elected in 1966")
- Nobel Prize in Physics (1970)
- Great Gold Medal of l’Électronique (1971)
- Great Gold Medal of the Société d’encouragement pour la recherche et l’invention (1973)
- Founding member of the [World Cultural Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Cultural_Council "World Cultural Council") (1981).

### Distinctions

Owing to his involvement in national defense, particularly through research in the protection of warships by demagnetization against magnetic mines, he received numerous distinctions:

- [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour "Legion of Honour"):
  - Knight (for exceptional military services) (1940)
  - Officer (1951)
  - Commander (1958)
  - Grand Officer (1966)
  - Grand Cross (1974)
- [Croix de Guerre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croix_de_Guerre_1939–1945_(France) "Croix de Guerre 1939–1945 (France)") with Palm (1940)
- Commander of the [Ordre des Palmes Académiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_des_Palmes_Académiques "Ordre des Palmes Académiques") (1957)
- Knight of the Order of Social Merit (1963)
- Grand Cross of the [National Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Merit_(France) "National Order of Merit (France)") (1972)
- Honorary Admiral (French Navy)
