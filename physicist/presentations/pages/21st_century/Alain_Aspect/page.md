**Alain Jean Aspect** (French: [[aspɛ]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/French "Help:IPA/French") [ⓘ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Fr-Aspect.oga "File:Fr-Aspect.oga"); born 15 June 1947) is a French [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist "Physicist") noted for his experimental work on [quantum entanglement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_entanglement "Quantum entanglement"). [Aspect's experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect's_experiment "Aspect's experiment") (1980–1982) bears his name.

Aspect was awarded the 2022 [Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics "Nobel Prize in Physics"), jointly with [John Clauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Clauser "John Clauser") and [Anton Zeilinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Zeilinger "Anton Zeilinger"), "for experiments with [entangled photons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_entanglement "Quantum entanglement"), establishing the violation of [Bell inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_inequalities "Bell inequalities") and pioneering [quantum information science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_science "Quantum information science")".

In 2025 he was elected to the [Académie Française](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_Française "Académie Française").

## Education

Aspect is a graduate of the [École Normale Supérieure de Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure_de_Cachan "École Normale Supérieure de Cachan") (ENS Cachan, today part of [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University "Paris-Saclay University")). He passed the *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation "Agrégation")* in physics in 1969 and received his PhD degree in 1971 from the [École supérieure d'optique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_supérieure_d'optique "École supérieure d'optique") (later known as [Institut d'Optique Graduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_d'Optique_Graduate_School "Institut d'Optique Graduate School")) of [Université d'Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_d'Orsay "Université d'Orsay") (later known as [Université Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Sud "Université Paris-Sud")). He then taught for three years in [Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon "Cameroon") as a replacement for then compulsory military service.

In the early 1980s, while working on his [doctorat d'État](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_d'État "Doctorat d'État") (habilitation thesis), he performed the [Bell test experiments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_test_experiments "Bell test experiments") that showed that [Albert Einstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Einstein "Albert Einstein"), [Boris Podolsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Podolsky "Boris Podolsky") and [Nathan Rosen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Rosen "Nathan Rosen")'s putative *[reductio ad absurdum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductio_ad_absurdum "Reductio ad absurdum")* of [quantum mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_mechanics "Quantum mechanics"), namely that it implied '[ghostly action at a distance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_entanglement "Quantum entanglement")', did in fact appear to be realized when two [particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_particle "Elementary particle") were separated by an arbitrarily large distance (see [EPR paradox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPR_paradox "EPR paradox") and [Aspect's experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect's_experiment "Aspect's experiment")). A correlation between the particles' [wave functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_function "Wave function") remains, as long as they were once part of the same undisturbed wave function before one of the child particles was measured. He defended his [doctorat d'État](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_d'État "Doctorat d'État") in 1983 at Université Paris-Sud (today part of Paris-Saclay University).

Aspect received an honorary doctorate from [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University "Heriot-Watt University") in 2008.

## Research

Aspect's experiments, following the first experiment of [Stuart Freedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_Freedman "Stuart Freedman") and [John Clauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Clauser "John Clauser") in 1972, were considered to provide further support to the thesis that [Bell's inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell's_theorem "Bell's theorem") are violated in its [CHSH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CHSH_Bell_test "CHSH Bell test") version, in particular by closing a form of the [locality loophole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_test_loopholes "Bell test loopholes"). However, his results were not completely conclusive since there were loopholes that allowed for alternative explanations that comply with [local realism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principle_of_locality "Principle of locality").

After his work on Bell's inequalities, Aspect turned toward studies of [laser cooling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_cooling "Laser cooling") of neutral atoms, and [Bose–Einstein condensates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bose–Einstein_condensate "Bose–Einstein condensate") at the [Kastler-Brossel Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kastler-Brossel_Laboratory "Kastler-Brossel Laboratory").

## Distinctions

Aspect was elected a [Foreign Member of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_2015 "List of Fellows of the Royal Society elected in 2015") (ForMemRS) in 2015. His certificate of election reads:

> For his fundamental experiments in quantum optics and atomic physics. Alain Aspect was the first to exclude subluminal communication between the measurement stations in experimental demonstrations that quantum mechanics invalidates separable hidden-variable theories and the first to demonstrate experimentally the wave–particle duality of single photons. He co-invented the technique of velocity-selective coherent population trapping, was the first to compare the [Hanbury Brown-Twiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanbury_Brown_and_Twiss_effect "Hanbury Brown and Twiss effect") correlations of fermions and bosons under the same conditions, and the first to demonstrate Anderson localization in an ultra-cold atom system. His experiments illuminate fundamental aspects of the quantum-mechanical behaviour of single photons, photon pairs and atoms.

In 2005 he was awarded the gold medal of the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique "Centre national de la recherche scientifique"), where he is Research Director. The 2010 [Wolf Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize "Wolf Prize") in physics was awarded to Aspect, [Anton Zeilinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Zeilinger "Anton Zeilinger") and [John Clauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Clauser "John Clauser"). In 2013 Aspect was awarded both the [Niels Bohr International Gold Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niels_Bohr_International_Gold_Medal "Niels Bohr International Gold Medal") and the [UNESCO Niels Bohr Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Niels_Bohr_Medal "UNESCO Niels Bohr Medal"). In 2011, he was assigned the [Medal of the City of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_of_the_City_of_Paris "Medal of the City of Paris"). In 2013, he was also awarded the [Balzan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balzan_Prize "Balzan Prize") for Quantum Information Processing and Communication. In 2014, he was named Officer of the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour "Legion of Honour").

Asteroid [33163 Alainaspect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/33163_Alainaspect "33163 Alainaspect"), discovered by astronomers at [Caussols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_de_recherches_en_géodynamique_et_astrométrie "Centre de recherches en géodynamique et astrométrie") in 1998, was named after him. The official [naming citation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meanings_of_minor-planet_names:_33001–34000#m163 "Meanings of minor-planet names: 33001–34000") was published by the [Minor Planet Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_Planet_Center "Minor Planet Center") on 8 November 2019 ([M.P.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_Planet_Circulars "Minor Planet Circulars") 118220).

Aspect was awarded the 2022 [Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics "Nobel Prize in Physics") alongside [John F. Clauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Clauser "John Clauser") and [Anton Zeilinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Zeilinger "Anton Zeilinger") "for experiments with entangled [photons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photon "Photon"), establishing the violation of [Bell's inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell's_theorem "Bell's theorem") and pioneering quantum information science".

On 26 June 2025, he was elected to the [Académie Française](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_Française "Académie Française").
