**George Elwood Smith** (May 10, 1930 – May 28, 2025) was an American applied [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist "Physicist") and a co-inventor of the [charge-coupled device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charge-coupled_device "Charge-coupled device") (CCD). Smith shared one half of the 2009 [Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics "Nobel Prize in Physics") with [Willard Boyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Boyle "Willard Boyle") "for the invention of an imaging semiconductor circuit - the CCD sensor."

## Education

George Elwood Smith was born on May 10, 1930, in [White Plains, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Plains,_New_York "White Plains, New York"). After serving in the [United States Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy "United States Navy") for four years and taking courses at the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami "University of Miami"), Smith qualified as a sophomore at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania "University of Pennsylvania") in 1952, graduating with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S. "B.S.") in 1955. He then became a teaching assistant at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago "University of Chicago"), where he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D. "Ph.D.") in 1959 with a thesis on the [anomalous skin effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skin_effect#Anomalous_skin_effect "Skin effect") in [bismuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bismuth "Bismuth").

## Career

From 1959 until his retirement in 1986, Smith worked at [Bell Telephone Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Telephone_Laboratories "Bell Telephone Laboratories") in [Murray Hill, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_Hill,_New_Jersey "Murray Hill, New Jersey"), where he led research into novel lasers and semiconductor devices. During his tenure, Smith was awarded dozens of patents and eventually headed the [VLSI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VLSI "VLSI") device department.

In 1969, Smith and [Willard Boyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Boyle "Willard Boyle") invented the [charge-coupled device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charge-coupled_device "Charge-coupled device") (CCD), for which they have jointly received the [Franklin Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Institute "Franklin Institute")'s [Stuart Ballantine Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_Ballantine_Medal "Stuart Ballantine Medal") in 1973, the 1974 [IEEE Morris N. Liebmann Memorial Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Morris_N._Liebmann_Memorial_Award "IEEE Morris N. Liebmann Memorial Award"), the 2006 [Charles Stark Draper Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Stark_Draper_Prize "Charles Stark Draper Prize"), and the 2009 [Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics "Nobel Prize in Physics").

## Personal life and death

Both Boyle and Smith were avid sailors who took many trips together. After retirement, Smith sailed around the world with his life partner, Janet, for seventeen years, eventually giving up his hobby in 2003 to "spare his 'creaky bones' from further storms". He resided in the [Waretown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waretown,_New_Jersey "Waretown, New Jersey") section of [Ocean Township, Ocean County, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean_Township,_Ocean_County,_New_Jersey "Ocean Township, Ocean County, New Jersey").

Smith died at his home in [Barnegat Township, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnegat_Township,_New_Jersey "Barnegat Township, New Jersey"), on May 28, 2025, at the age of 95.
