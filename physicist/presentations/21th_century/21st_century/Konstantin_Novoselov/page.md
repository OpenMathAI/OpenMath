**Sir Konstantin Sergeevich Novoselov** (Russian: Константи́н Серге́евич Новосёлов, IPA: [[kənstɐnʲˈtʲin sʲɪrˈɡʲe(j)ɪvʲɪtɕ nəvɐˈsʲɵləf]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Russian "Help:IPA/Russian"); born 23 August 1974) is a Russian–British physicist. His work on [graphene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphene "Graphene") with [Andre Geim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andre_Geim "Andre Geim") earned them the [Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics "Nobel Prize in Physics") in 2010. Novoselov is a professor at the [Centre for Advanced 2D Materials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Advanced_2D_Materials "Centre for Advanced 2D Materials"), [National University of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Singapore "National University of Singapore") and is also the [Langworthy Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langworthy_Professor "Langworthy Professor") of the [School of Physics and Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Physics_and_Astronomy,_University_of_Manchester "School of Physics and Astronomy, University of Manchester") at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester "University of Manchester"), and serves as President of [Constructor University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructor_University "Constructor University") in Bremen, Germany.

## Education

Konstantin Novoselov was born in [Nizhny Tagil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhny_Tagil "Nizhny Tagil"), [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union "Soviet Union"), in 1974. He graduated from the [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology "Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology") with a [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science "Master of Science") degree in 1997, and was awarded a PhD from the [Radboud University of Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_of_Nijmegen "Radboud University of Nijmegen") in 2004 for work supervised by [Andre Geim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andre_Geim "Andre Geim").

Novoselov uses the nickname "Kostya" ([diminutive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diminutive "Diminutive") of the name [Konstantin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konstantin "Konstantin")).

## Career

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d4/Konstantin_Novoselov_in_lab.jpg/250px-Konstantin_Novoselov_in_lab.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Konstantin_Novoselov_in_lab.jpg)

Konstantin Novoselov in his lab

Novoselov has published 475 [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review "Peer review") research papers on several topics including mesoscopic [superconductivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superconductivity "Superconductivity") ([Hall magnetometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall_effect "Hall effect")) as of January 2024 subatomic movements of [magnetic domain walls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_wall_(magnetism) "Domain wall (magnetism)"), the discovery of [gecko tape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gecko_tape "Gecko tape") and [graphene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphene "Graphene").,

Kostya Novoselov participated in the [Graphene Flagship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphene_Flagship "Graphene Flagship") project – a €1 billion initiative of the European Commission – and was featured in the official promotion movie of the project.

Novoselov was the first Director of the [National Graphene Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Graphene_Institute "National Graphene Institute") and sits on the International Scientific Advisory Committee of Australia's [ARC Centre of Excellence in Future Low-Energy Electronics Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Centre_of_Excellence_in_Future_Low-Energy_Electronics_Technologies "ARC Centre of Excellence in Future Low-Energy Electronics Technologies").

Novoselov was also a recipient of a starting grant from the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council "European Research Council").

Kostya Novoselov made it into a shortlist of scientists with multiple hot papers for the years 2007–2008 (shared second place with 13 hot papers) and 2009 (5th place with 12 hot papers).

In 2014 Kostya Novoselov was included in the list of the most highly cited researchers. He was also named among the 17 hottest researchers worldwide—"individuals who have published the greatest number of hot papers during 2012–2013".

Novoselov joined the National University of Singapore's Centre for Advanced 2D Materials in 2019, making him the first Nobel laureate to join a Singaporean university. In 2021, he pioneered with [Antonio H. Castro Neto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_H._Castro_Neto "Antonio H. Castro Neto") a new research centre at the [National University of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Singapore "National University of Singapore") called IFIM. With $200 million in funding over 10 years provided by the [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_(Singapore) "Ministry of Education (Singapore)") and NUS, the centre will work on making ground-breaking discoveries into what are called functional intelligent materials.

## National Graphene Institute

Novoselov led the academic team which overviewed the design, construction and launching of the [National Graphene Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Graphene_Institute "National Graphene Institute"). He contributed with a number of unique architectural and technical solutions. The veil of the National Graphene Institute depicts formulae from his and Prof. A. Geim early works on graphene. Also, Novoselov confirms that among the formulae several scientific jokes are hidden, though he has never revealed them.

He co-authored a book on the architecture of the National Graphene Institute.

## Other projects

In 2018, in a project of exploration of the archives of the Jodrell Bank Observatory, Prof. Novoselov helped Prof. Tim O'Brian to transcribe radio transmission (most possibly simulated instrument reading) from the Soviet [Zond 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zond_6 "Zond 6") received by radio telescope at [Jodrell Bank Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodrell_Bank_Observatory "Jodrell Bank Observatory") in November 1968.,

## Art involvement

Novoselov is known for his interest in art. He practices in Chinese traditional drawing and has been involved in several projects on modern art.
Thus, in February 2015 he combined forces with [Cornelia Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornelia_Parker "Cornelia Parker") to create a display for the opening of the [Whitworth Art Gallery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitworth_Art_Gallery "Whitworth Art Gallery"). Cornelia Parker's meteorite shower firework (pieces of meteorites loaded in firework) was launched by Novoselov breathing on graphene gas sensor (which changed the resistance of graphene due to doping by water vapour). [Graphene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphene "Graphene") was obtained through exfoliation of [graphite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphite "Graphite") which was extracted from a drawing of [William Blake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Blake "William Blake"). Novoselov suggested that he also exfoliated graphite obtained from the drawings of other prominent artists: [John Constable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Constable "John Constable"), [Pablo Picasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Picasso "Pablo Picasso"), [J. M. W. Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._M._W._Turner "J. M. W. Turner"), [Thomas Girtin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Girtin "Thomas Girtin"). He said that only microscopic amounts (flake size less than 100 micrometres) was extracted from each of the drawings. In 2015 he participated in "in conversation" session with [Douglas Gordon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Gordon "Douglas Gordon") during Interdependence session at [Manchester International Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_International_Festival "Manchester International Festival").

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0f/Konstantin_Novoselov_Chinese_painting_2015.jpg/250px-Konstantin_Novoselov_Chinese_painting_2015.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Konstantin_Novoselov_Chinese_painting_2015.jpg)

Novoselov while painting at the residence of Chinese Consul General Li in Manchester.

He also participates in discussions on the relation between art and science. Novoselov believes that artists and scientists both rely on curiosity, willingness to learn and imagination:

> Artists and scientists both think outside the box. They've got to come with genius experiments or ideas to expose the most interesting phenomena. Later, they've got to diverge a little bit because scientists will start to look at the common elements between many of the phenomena to describe the most general law, and artists will probably try to study individuals rather than the crowd as a whole. But we're just two sides of the same medal.

Novoselov is fond of Chinese calligraphy and drawing. He learned it from a prominent Chinese artist [Zheng Shenglong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_Shenglong?action=edit&redlink=1 "Zheng Shenglong"). Nine ink paintings by Prof. Novoselov were shown at the exhibition "Britain Through the Eyes of a Chinese Diplomat" at the University of Leeds.
One of his paintings is now in the collection of [Xi Jinping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi_Jinping "Xi Jinping"), the [General Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Secretary_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party "General Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party") since 2012.

Novoselov participated in [Viennacontemporary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viennacontemporary "Viennacontemporary") in 2017, where 5 of his works have been presented by RDI.Creative gallery. The paintings presented a range of topics, from the very traditional Chinese paintings to landscapes to contemporary subjects. It is claimed that graphene ink has been used in at least some of those paintings.

In 2025, Novoselov curated an exhibition at [Venice Biennale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Biennale "Venice Biennale").

## Personal life

Novoselov holds both Russian and British citizenship. He is married and has two daughters. He is an agnostic.

## Political positions

In February 2022, he signed an open letter by Russian scientists condemning the [2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine "2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine").
