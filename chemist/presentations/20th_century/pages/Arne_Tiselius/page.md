**Arne Wilhelm Kaurin Tiselius** (10 August 1902 – 29 October 1971) was a Swedish [biochemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biochemist "Biochemist") who won the [Nobel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize "Nobel Prize") in [Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Chemistry "Nobel Prize in Chemistry") in 1948 "for his research on [electrophoresis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrophoresis "Electrophoresis") and [adsorption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adsorption "Adsorption") analysis, especially for his discoveries concerning the complex nature of the [serum proteins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serum_protein_electrophoresis "Serum protein electrophoresis")."

## Education

Tiselius was born in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm "Stockholm"). Following the death of his father, the family moved to [Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg "Gothenburg") where he went to school, and after graduation at the local "Realgymnasium" in 1921, he studied at the [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University "Uppsala University"), specializing in chemistry.

## Career and research

Tiselius became a research assistant at [Theodor Svedberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Svedberg "Theodor Svedberg")'s laboratory in 1925 and obtained his doctoral degree in 1930 on "The Moving Boundary Method of Studying the [Electrophoresis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moving-boundary_electrophoresis "Moving-boundary electrophoresis") of Proteins". From then to 1935 he published a number of papers on [diffusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffusion "Diffusion") and [adsorption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adsorption "Adsorption") in naturally occurring base-exchanging [zeolites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeolites "Zeolites"), and these studies continued during a year's visit to [Hugh Stott Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Stott_Taylor "Hugh Stott Taylor")'s laboratory in [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University "Princeton University") with support of a [Rockefeller Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_Foundation "Rockefeller Foundation") fellowship. On his return to [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala "Uppsala") he resumed his interest in proteins, and the application of physical methods to [biochemical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biochemistry "Biochemistry") problems. This led to a much-improved method of electrophoretic analysis which he refined in subsequent years.

Tiselius took an active part in the reorganization of scientific research in Sweden in the years following World War II, and was President of the [International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Pure_and_Applied_Chemistry "International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry") 1951–1955. He was chairman of the board for the [Nobel Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Foundation "Nobel Foundation") from 1960 to 1964.

## Awards and honours

- 1948 [Nobel Prize for Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_for_Chemistry "Nobel Prize for Chemistry")
- 1949 Elected a [Foreign Associate of the National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Associate_of_the_National_Academy_of_Sciences "Foreign Associate of the National Academy of Sciences")
- 1953 Elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences "American Academy of Arts and Sciences")
- 1957 Elected a [Foreign Member of the Royal Society (ForMemRS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_1957 "List of Fellows of the Royal Society elected in 1957").
- 1961 [Paul Karrer Gold Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Karrer_Gold_Medal "Paul Karrer Gold Medal")
- 1964 Elected to the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society "American Philosophical Society")

The lunar crater [Tiselius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiselius_(crater) "Tiselius (crater)") was named in his honour.

## Personal life

Tiselius was married, with two children. He died of a heart attack 29 October 1971 in [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala "Uppsala").
