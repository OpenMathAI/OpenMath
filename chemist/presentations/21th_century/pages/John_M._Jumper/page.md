**John Michael Jumper** (born 1 January 1985) is an American chemist and computer scientist. Jumper, [Demis Hassabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demis_Hassabis "Demis Hassabis") and [David Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Baker_(biochemist) "David Baker (biochemist)") were awarded the 2024 [Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Chemistry "Nobel Prize in Chemistry") for [protein structure prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_structure_prediction "Protein structure prediction").

Jumper served as a director at [Google DeepMind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepMind "DeepMind") for nearly nine years. Jumper and his colleagues created [AlphaFold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AlphaFold "AlphaFold"), an [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence") (AI) model to predict [protein structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_structure "Protein structure") from their [amino acid sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amino_acid_sequence "Amino acid sequence") with high accuracy. The AlphaFold team had released 214 million protein structures as of January 2024.

The scientific journal *[Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_(journal) "Nature (journal)")* included Jumper as one of the ten "people who mattered" in science in their annual listing of [*Nature*'s 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature's_10 "Nature's 10") in 2021.

In June 2026, Jumper announced that he would leave the company to join [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic "Anthropic") after a break.

## Education

Jumper graduated from [Pulaski Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulaski_Academy "Pulaski Academy") in 2003. He received a Bachelor of Science with majors in physics and mathematics from [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University "Vanderbilt University") in 2007, a [Master of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Philosophy "Master of Philosophy") in [theoretical condensed matter physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_condensed_matter_physics "Theoretical condensed matter physics") from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge "University of Cambridge") where he was a student of [St Edmund's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Edmund's_College,_Cambridge "St Edmund's College, Cambridge") in 2008 on a [Marshall Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Scholarship "Marshall Scholarship"), a Master of Science in [theoretical chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_chemistry "Theoretical chemistry") from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago "University of Chicago") in 2012, and a Doctor of Philosophy in theoretical chemistry from the University of Chicago in 2017. His doctoral advisors at the University of Chicago were Tobin R. Sosnick and [Karl Freed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Freed "Karl Freed").

## Career

Jumper's research investigates algorithms for [protein structure prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_structure_prediction "Protein structure prediction").

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/The_performance_of_AlphaFold.png/250px-The_performance_of_AlphaFold.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_performance_of_AlphaFold.png)

This image represents the final product of [AlphaFold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AlphaFold "AlphaFold") and it compares its results with other competitors at the [CASP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CASP "CASP") competition.

[AlphaFold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AlphaFold "AlphaFold") is a [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning "Deep learning") algorithm developed by Jumper and his team at DeepMind, a research lab acquired by Google's parent company [Alphabet Inc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphabet_Inc "Alphabet Inc"). It is an artificial intelligence program which performs [predictions of protein structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_structure_prediction "Protein structure prediction").

In November 2020, AlphaFold was named the winner of the 14th [Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_Assessment_of_Structure_Prediction "Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction") (CASP) competition. This international competition benchmarks algorithms to determine which one can best predict the 3D structure of proteins. AlphaFold won the competition, outperforming other algorithms scoring above 90 for around two-thirds of the proteins in CASP's [global distance test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_distance_test "Global distance test") (GDT), a test that measures the degree to which a computational program predicted structure is similar to the lab experiment determined structure, with 100 being a complete match, within the distance cutoff used for calculating GDT.

In 2026, Jumper left DeepMind and joined [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic "Anthropic").

## Awards and honors

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f4/David_Baker%2C_Demis_Hassabis%2C_and_John_Jumper_at_2024_Nobel_Prize_Conference.jpg/250px-David_Baker%2C_Demis_Hassabis%2C_and_John_Jumper_at_2024_Nobel_Prize_Conference.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:David_Baker,_Demis_Hassabis,_and_John_Jumper_at_2024_Nobel_Prize_Conference.jpg)

David Baker, Demis Hassabis, and John Jumper at 2024 Nobel Prize Conference

In 2021, Jumper was awarded the [BBVA Foundation Frontiers of Knowledge Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBVA_Foundation_Frontiers_of_Knowledge_Award "BBVA Foundation Frontiers of Knowledge Award") in the category "Biology and Biomedicine". In 2022 Jumper received the [Wiley Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiley_Prize "Wiley Prize") in Biomedical Sciences and for 2023 the [Breakthrough Prize in Life Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakthrough_Prize_in_Life_Sciences "Breakthrough Prize in Life Sciences") for developing AlphaFold, which accurately predicts the structure of a protein. In 2023 he was awarded the [Canada Gairdner International Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Gairdner_International_Award "Canada Gairdner International Award") and the [Albert Lasker Award for Basic Medical Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Lasker_Award_for_Basic_Medical_Research "Albert Lasker Award for Basic Medical Research").

In 2024, Jumper and [Demis Hassabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demis_Hassabis "Demis Hassabis") shared half of the [Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Chemistry "Nobel Prize in Chemistry") for their protein folding predictions, the other half went to [David Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Baker_(biochemist) "David Baker (biochemist)") for computational [protein design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_design "Protein design").

In 2025, Jumper received the Golden Plate Award of the [American Academy of Achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Achievement "Academy of Achievement") and the Marshall Medal of the [Marshall Aid Commemoration Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Aid_Commemoration_Commission "Marshall Aid Commemoration Commission"). He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society "Fellow of the Royal Society") (FRS) that same year. In 2026, he was elected a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering "National Academy of Engineering").
