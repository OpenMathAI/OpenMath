**Avram Hershko** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language "Hebrew language"): אברהם הרשקו, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew "Romanization of Hebrew"): *Avraham Hershko*, [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language "Hungarian language"): *Herskó Ferenc Ábrahám*; born December 31, 1937) is a Hungarian-born Israeli [biochemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology "Biology") who received the [Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Chemistry "Nobel Prize in Chemistry") in 2004.

## Biography

He was born **Herskó Ferenc** in [Karcag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karcag "Karcag"), [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary_(1920–1946) "Kingdom of Hungary (1920–1946)"), into a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews "Jews") family, the son of Shoshana/Margit 'Manci' (née Wulc) and Moshe Hershko, both teachers.
During the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War "Second World War"), his father was forced into [labor service in the Hungarian army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_service_in_Hungary_during_World_War_II "Labour service in Hungary during World War II") and then taken as a prisoner by the [Soviet Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Army "Soviet Army"). For years, Avram's family didn't know anything about what had happened to his father. Avram, his mother and older brother Chaim/Laszlo 'Laci' were put in a [ghetto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghetto "Ghetto") in [Szolnok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szolnok "Szolnok"). During the final days of the ghetto, most Jews were sent to be murdered in [Auschwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitz_concentration_camp "Auschwitz concentration camp"), but Avram and his family managed to board trains that took them to a [concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_concentration_camps "Nazi concentration camps") in [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria_under_National_Socialism "Austria under National Socialism"), where they were forced into labor until the end of the war. Avram and his mother and brother survived the war and returned to their home. His father returned as well, 4 years after they had last seen him.

Hershko and his family emigrated to Israel in 1950 and settled in [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem "Jerusalem"). He received his MD in 1965 and his PhD in 1969 from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem "Hebrew University of Jerusalem")-[Hadassah Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadassah_Medical_Center "Hadassah Medical Center"). He was a [postdoctoral scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher "Postdoctoral researcher") at the [University of California, San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Francisco "University of California, San Francisco"). He is currently a Distinguished Professor at the Rappaport Faculty of Medicine at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology "Technion – Israel Institute of Technology") in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa "Haifa") and a Distinguished Adjunct Professor at the [New York University Grossman School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Grossman_School_of_Medicine "New York University Grossman School of Medicine").

Along with [Aaron Ciechanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Ciechanover "Aaron Ciechanover") and [Irwin Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irwin_Rose "Irwin Rose"), he was awarded the 2004 [Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Chemistry "Nobel Prize in Chemistry") for the discovery of [ubiquitin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubiquitin "Ubiquitin")-mediated [protein degradation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proteolysis#Protein_degradation "Proteolysis"). The ubiquitin-[proteasome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proteasome "Proteasome") system has a critical role in maintaining the homeostasis of cells and is believed to be involved in the development and progression of diseases such as cancer, muscular and neurological diseases, and immune and inflammatory responses.

His contributions to science directly helped cure one of his long-time friends of cancer.

## Honours and awards

- 1987 – [Weizmann Prize for Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Prize_for_Research_in_the_Exact_Sciences?action=edit&redlink=1 "Weizmann Prize for Research in the Exact Sciences")
- 1994 – [Israel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prize "Israel Prize") in Biochemistry
- 1999 – [Canada Gairdner International Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Gairdner_International_Award "Canada Gairdner International Award") (with [Alexander Varshavsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Varshavsky "Alexander Varshavsky"))
- 2000 – [Albert Lasker Award for Basic Medical Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Lasker_Award_for_Basic_Medical_Research "Albert Lasker Award for Basic Medical Research") (with [Aaron Ciechanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Ciechanover "Aaron Ciechanover") and Varshavsky)
- 2000 – [Alfred P. Sloan Jr. Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_P._Sloan_Jr._Prize "Alfred P. Sloan Jr. Prize") (with Varshavsky)
- 2000 – Member, [Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities "Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities")
- 2001 – [Louisa Gross Horwitz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisa_Gross_Horwitz_Prize "Louisa Gross Horwitz Prize") from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University") (with Varshavsky)
- 2001 – [Massry Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massry_Prize "Massry Prize") from the [Keck School of Medicine of USC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keck_School_of_Medicine_of_USC "Keck School of Medicine of USC"), [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California "University of Southern California") (with Varshavsky)
- 2001 – [Wolf Prize in Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize_in_Medicine "Wolf Prize in Medicine") (with Varshavsky) for "the discovery of the [ubiquitin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubiquitin "Ubiquitin") system of intracellular [protein degradation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proteolysis#Protein_degradation "Proteolysis") and the crucial functions of this system in cellular regulation."
- 2002 – [The EMET Prize for Art, Science and Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_EMET_Prize_for_Art,_Science_and_Culture "The EMET Prize for Art, Science and Culture") in the category of Life Sciences (with Ciechanover and [Leo Sachs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Sachs "Leo Sachs"))
- 2002 – [E.B. Wilson Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.B._Wilson_Medal "E.B. Wilson Medal") (with Varshavsky)
- 2003 – Foreign Associate, [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences "National Academy of Sciences"), USA
- 2004 – Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his discovery with Ciechanover and [Irwin Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irwin_Rose "Irwin Rose"), of ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation
- 2005 – Elected to the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society "American Philosophical Society")
