**Joachim Frank** (German pronunciation: [[ˈjoːaxɪm ˈfʁaŋk]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Standard_German "Help:IPA/Standard German") [ⓘ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:De-Joachim_Frank.ogg "File:De-Joachim Frank.ogg")) [HonFRMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_Fellow_of_the_Royal_Microscopical_Society "Honorary Fellow of the Royal Microscopical Society"); born September 12, 1940) is a German-American [biophysicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biophysics "Biophysics") at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University") and a Nobel laureate. He is regarded as the founder of single-particle [cryo-electron microscopy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryo-electron_microscopy "Cryo-electron microscopy") (cryo-EM), for which he shared the [Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Chemistry "Nobel Prize in Chemistry") in 2017 with [Jacques Dubochet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Dubochet "Jacques Dubochet") and [Richard Henderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Henderson_(biologist) "Richard Henderson (biologist)"). He also made significant contributions to structure and function of the [ribosome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribosome "Ribosome") from bacteria and eukaryotes.

## Life and career

Frank was born in [Siegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegen "Siegen") in the borough of [Weidenau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weidenau "Weidenau"). After completing his Vordiplom (B.S.) degree in physics at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg "University of Freiburg") (1963) and his [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom "Diplom") under [Walter Rollwagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Rollwagen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Walter Rollwagen")'s mentorship at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich "LMU Munich") with the thesis "Untersuchung der Sekundärelektronen-Emission von Gold am Schmelzpunkt" (Investigation of secondary electron emission of gold at its melting point) (1967), Frank obtained his Ph.D. from the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich "Technical University of Munich") for graduate studies in [Walter Hoppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Hoppe "Walter Hoppe")'s lab at the Max Planck Institut für Eiweiss- und Lederforschung (now [Max Planck Institute of Biochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_of_Biochemistry "Max Planck Institute of Biochemistry")) with the dissertation *Untersuchungen von elektronenmikroskopischen Aufnahmen hoher Auflösung mit Bilddifferenz- und Rekonstruktionsverfahren* (Investigations of high-resolution electron micrographs using image difference and reconstruction methods) (1970). The thesis explores the use of digital image processing and optical diffraction in the analysis of electron micrographs, and alignment of images using the cross-correlation function.

As a [Harkness postdoctoral fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harkness_Fellowship "Harkness Fellowship"), he had the opportunity to study for two years in the United States: with Robert Nathan at the [Jet Propulsion Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_Propulsion_Laboratory "Jet Propulsion Laboratory"), [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology "California Institute of Technology"); with [Robert M. Glaeser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_M._Glaeser?action=edit&redlink=1 "Robert M. Glaeser") at Donner Lab, [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley "University of California, Berkeley") and with [Benjamin M. Siegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_M._Siegel?action=edit&redlink=1 "Benjamin M. Siegel") at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University "Cornell University"), Ithaca, New York. In the fall of 1972 he returned briefly to the [Max Planck Institute of Biochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_of_Biochemistry "Max Planck Institute of Biochemistry") in Martinsried as research assistant, working on the theory of partial coherence in electron microscopy, then, in 1973, he joined the [Cavendish Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavendish_Laboratory "Cavendish Laboratory"), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge "University of Cambridge") as Senior Research Assistant under [Vernon Ellis Cosslett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vernon_Ellis_Cosslett "Vernon Ellis Cosslett").

In 1975 Frank was offered a position of senior research scientist in the Division of Laboratories and Research (now [Wadsworth Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadsworth_Center "Wadsworth Center")), New York State Department of Health, where he started working on single-particle approaches in electron microscopy. In 1985 he was appointed associate and then (1986) full professor at the newly formed Department of Biomedical Sciences of the [University at Albany, State University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Albany,_SUNY "University at Albany, SUNY"). In 1987 and 1994, he went on sabbaticals in Europe, one to work with [Richard Henderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Henderson_(biologist) "Richard Henderson (biologist)"), [Laboratory of Molecular Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laboratory_of_Molecular_Biology "Laboratory of Molecular Biology") Medical Research Council in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge "Cambridge") and the other as a [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Research_Award "Humboldt Research Award") winner with [Kenneth C. Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Holmes "Kenneth Holmes"), [Max Planck Institute for Medical Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_for_Medical_Research "Max Planck Institute for Medical Research") in [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg "Heidelberg"). In 1998 Frank was appointed investigator of the [Howard Hughes Medical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Hughes_Medical_Institute "Howard Hughes Medical Institute") (HHMI). Since 2003 he was also lecturer at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University"), and he joined [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University") in 2008 as professor of
[Biochemistry and Molecular Biophysics](http://www.wikicu.com/Biochemistry_and_Molecular_Biophysics_Department) and of biological sciences.
