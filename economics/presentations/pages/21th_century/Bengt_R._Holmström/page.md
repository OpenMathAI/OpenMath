**Bengt Robert Holmström** (born 18 April 1949) is a Finnish [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist "Economist") who is currently Paul A. Samuelson Professor of Economics (Emeritus) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology "Massachusetts Institute of Technology"). Together with [Oliver Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Hart_(economist) "Oliver Hart (economist)"), he received the Central Bank of Sweden [Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Memorial_Prize_in_Economic_Sciences "Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences") in 2016.

## Early life and education

Holmström was born in [Helsinki, Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki,_Finland "Helsinki, Finland"), on 18 April 1949, and belongs to the [Swedish speaking minority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland-Swedes "Finland-Swedes") of Finland. He received his [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science "Bachelor of Science") in mathematics and science from the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki "University of Helsinki") in 1972. He also received a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science "Master of Science") degree in [operations research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research "Operations Research") from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University "Stanford University") in 1975.

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D. "Ph.D.") from the [Graduate School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Graduate_School_of_Business "Stanford Graduate School of Business") at Stanford in 1978. He moved to the United States in 1976.

## Career

He worked as a corporate planner from 1972 until 1974, then was an assistant professor at the [Hanken School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanken_School_of_Economics "Hanken School of Economics") from 1978 until 1979. He served as an associate professor at the [Kellogg Graduate School of Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kellogg_Graduate_School_of_Management "Kellogg Graduate School of Management") at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University "Northwestern University") (1979–1983) and as the Edwin J. Beinecke Professor of Management at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University "Yale University")’s School of Management (1983–1994). Holmström was elected Alumnus of The Year by the University of Helsinki Alumni Association in 2010.

He has been on the faculty of M.I.T. since 1994, when he was appointed professor of economics and management at the department of economics and Sloan School of Management.

Holmström is particularly well known for his work on [principal-agent theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal-agent_theory "Principal-agent theory"). His work made seminal advances in understanding contracting in the presence of uncertainty. More generally, he has worked on the theory of contracting and incentives especially as applied to the [theory of the firm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_the_firm "Theory of the firm"), to corporate governance and to liquidity problems in financial crises. He praised the taxpayer-backed [bailouts by the US government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_Economic_Stabilization_Act_of_2008 "Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008") during the [2008 financial crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_financial_crisis "2008 financial crisis") and emphasizes the benefits of opacity in the [money market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_market "Money market").

Holmström was elected member of the [Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters "Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters") in 1992 and an honorary member of the same society in 2016. He is a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences "American Academy of Arts and Sciences"), the [Econometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Econometric_Society "Econometric Society"), the [European Economic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Association "European Economic Association") and the [American Finance Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Finance_Association "American Finance Association"), and a foreign member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences "Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences") and the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters "Finnish Academy of Science and Letters"). In 2011, he served as President of the Econometric Society. He holds honorary doctorate degrees from the [Stockholm School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_School_of_Economics "Stockholm School of Economics"), Sweden, the [University of Vaasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vaasa "University of Vaasa") and the [Hanken School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanken_School_of_Economics "Hanken School of Economics") in Finland.

Holmström was a member of [Nokia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia "Nokia")'s [board of directors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_directors "Board of directors") from 1999 until 2012. He was a member of the Board of the [Aalto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalto_University "Aalto University") from 2008 until 2017.

## Accolades

He was awarded the 2012 Banque de France-TSE Senior Prize in Monetary Economics and Finance, the 2013 Stephen A. Ross Prize in Financial Economics and the 2013 Chicago Mercantile Exchange – MSRI Prize for Innovative Quantitative Applications.

In 2016, Holmström won the [Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sveriges_Riksbank_Prize_in_Economic_Sciences_in_Memory_of_Alfred_Nobel "Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel") together with [Oliver Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Hart_(economist) "Oliver Hart (economist)") "for their contributions to [contract theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_theory "Contract theory")".

## Personal life

He is married to Anneli Holmström and they have one son.
