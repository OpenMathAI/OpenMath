**Robert Fry Engle III** (born November 10, 1942) is an American economist and [statistician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistician "Statistician"). He was awarded the 2003 [Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Memorial_Prize_in_Economic_Sciences "Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences"), sharing the award with [Clive Granger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clive_Granger "Clive Granger"), "for methods of analyzing economic [time series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series "Time series") with time-varying [volatility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volatility_(finance) "Volatility (finance)") ([ARCH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoregressive_conditional_heteroskedasticity "Autoregressive conditional heteroskedasticity"))".

## Biography

Engle was born in [Syracuse, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse,_New_York "Syracuse, New York") into a [Quaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaker "Quaker") family and went on to graduate from [Williams College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_College "Williams College") with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science "Bachelor of Science") in physics. He earned a [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science "Master of Science") in physics and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy "Doctor of Philosophy") in economics, both from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University "Cornell University"), in 1966 and 1969 respectively. After completing his PhD, Engle became an economics professor at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology "Massachusetts Institute of Technology") from 1969 to 1977. He joined the faculty of the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego "University of California, San Diego") (UCSD) in 1975, wherefrom he retired in 2003. He now holds positions of professor emeritus and research professor at UCSD. He currently teaches at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University "New York University"), [Stern School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stern_School_of_Business "Stern School of Business") where he is the Michael Armellino professor in Management of Financial Services. At [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University "New York University"), Engle teaches for the Master of Science in Risk Management Program for Executives.

Engle's most important contribution was his path-breaking discovery of a method for analyzing unpredictable movements in financial market prices and [interest rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interest_rates "Interest rates"). Accurate characterization and prediction of these volatile movements are essential for quantifying and effectively managing [risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk "Risk"). For example, risk measurement plays a key role in pricing [options](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Option_(finance) "Option (finance)") and [financial derivatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_derivatives "Financial derivatives"). Previous researchers had either assumed constant [volatility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volatility_(finance) "Volatility (finance)") or had used simple devices to approximate it. Engle developed new statistical models of volatility that captured the tendency of stock prices and other financial variables to move between high volatility and low volatility periods ("Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity: ARCH"). These statistical models have become essential tools of modern [arbitrage pricing theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbitrage_pricing_theory "Arbitrage pricing theory") and practice.

Engle was the central founder and director of NYU-Stern's Volatility Institute which publishes weekly data on [systemic risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systemic_risk "Systemic risk") across countries on its V-LAB site. He was awarded a [Doctor Honoris Causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Honoris_Causa "Doctor Honoris Causa") by the [Comillas Pontifical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comillas_Pontifical_University "Comillas Pontifical University") in Spain in 2024.

## Personal life

In August 1969, Engle married Marianne Eger. They have two children, a daughter and a son. His wife’s mother is [Edith Eger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edith_Eger "Edith Eger"), a clinical psychologist, author, and survivor of the Holocaust.
