**Sir Michael Houghton** (born 1949) is a British scientist and Nobel Prize laureate. Along with [Qui-Lim Choo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qui-Lim_Choo "Qui-Lim Choo"), [George Kuo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Kuo "George Kuo") and [Daniel W. Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_W._Bradley "Daniel W. Bradley"), he co-discovered [hepatitis C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepatitis_C "Hepatitis C") in 1989. He also co-discovered the [hepatitis D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepatitis_D "Hepatitis D") [genome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genome "Genome") in 1986. The discovery of the [hepatitis C virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepatitis_C_virus "Hepatitis C virus") (HCV) led to the rapid development of diagnostic reagents to detect HCV in blood supplies, which has reduced the risk of acquiring HCV through blood transfusion from one in three to about one in two million. It is estimated that antibody testing has prevented at least 40,000 new infections per year in the US alone and many more worldwide.

Houghton is currently Canada Excellence Research Chair in [Virology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virology "Virology") and Li Ka Shing Professor of Virology at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta "University of Alberta"), where he is also director of the Li Ka Shing Applied Virology Institute. He was the co-recipient of the 2020 [Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physiology_or_Medicine "Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine") along with [Harvey J. Alter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_J._Alter "Harvey J. Alter") and [Charles M. Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_M._Rice "Charles M. Rice").

## Early life and education

Born in London in 1949 to Leonard George and Elsie Cressy Houghton, he was raised in a working-class family along with elder brother Graham. His father was a truck driver and union official. He received his primary education at Lyndhurst Grove School, and then won a scholarship to [Alleyn's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alleyn's_School "Alleyn's School") in [Dulwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulwich "Dulwich"), [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"), where he went on to specialise in physics, chemistry and maths. He won a scholarship to study at the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia "University of East Anglia"), graduating with a [lower second class honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_undergraduate_degree_classification#Lower_second-class_honours "British undergraduate degree classification") degree in [biological sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_sciences "Biological sciences") in 1972, and subsequently completed a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy "Doctor of Philosophy") in [biochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biochemistry "Biochemistry") from [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London "King's College London") in 1977.

## Career

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/15_Hegasy_Nobel_Prize_2020_HepC.jpg/330px-15_Hegasy_Nobel_Prize_2020_HepC.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:15_Hegasy_Nobel_Prize_2020_HepC.jpg)

Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2020: Seminal experiments by HJ Alter, M Houghton and CM Rice leading to the discovery of HCV as the causative agent of non-A, non-B hepatitis.

Houghton joined [G. D. Searle & Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._D._Searle_&_Company "G. D. Searle & Company") before moving to [Chiron Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiron_Corporation "Chiron Corporation") in 1982. It was at Chiron that Houghton together with colleagues [Qui-Lim Choo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qui-Lim_Choo "Qui-Lim Choo") and [George Kuo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Kuo "George Kuo"), and [Daniel W. Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_W._Bradley "Daniel W. Bradley") from the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention "Centers for Disease Control and Prevention"), first discovered evidence for HCV.

Houghton was co-author of a series of seminal studies published in 1989 and 1990 that identified hepatitis C antibodies in blood, particularly among patients at higher risk of contracting the disease, including those who had received blood transfusions. This work led to the development of a blood screening test in 1990; widespread blood screening that began in 1992 with the development of a more sensitive test has since virtually eliminated hepatitis C contamination of donated blood supplies in Canada. In other studies published during the same period, Houghton and collaborators linked hepatitis C with liver cancer.

In 2013, Houghton's team at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta "University of Alberta") showed that a vaccine derived from a single strain of Hepatitis C was effective against all strains of the virus. As of 2020, the vaccine was in [pre-clinical trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-clinical_trial "Pre-clinical trial").

## Honours and awards

- 1992 – [Karl Landsteiner Memorial Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Landsteiner_Memorial_Award "Karl Landsteiner Memorial Award")
- 1993 – [Robert Koch Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Koch_Prize "Robert Koch Prize")
- 1994 – [William Beaumont Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Beaumont_Prize "William Beaumont Prize")

- 2000 – [Lasker Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lasker_Award "Lasker Award")
- 2005 – Dale A. Smith Memorial Award
- 2009 – Hepdart Lifetime Achievement Award
- 2013 – He became the first person to decline the [$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_dollar "Canadian dollar")100,000 [Gairdner Foundation International Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gairdner_Foundation_International_Award "Gairdner Foundation International Award") stating "I felt that it would be unfair of me to accept this award without the inclusion of two colleagues, Dr. [Qui-Lim Choo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qui-Lim_Choo "Qui-Lim Choo") and Dr. [George Kuo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Kuo "George Kuo")."
- 2019 – Honorary doctorate of science from the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia "University of East Anglia")
- 2020 – [Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physiology_or_Medicine "Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine")
- 2021 – [Knighted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Bachelor "Knight Bachelor") in the [2021 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Birthday_Honours "2021 Birthday Honours") for services to medicine
- 2025 – Honorary doctorate of science from [Hong Kong Baptist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Baptist_University "Hong Kong Baptist University")
