**Sir Paul Maxime Nurse** (born 25 January 1949) is an English [geneticist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneticist "Geneticist"), [President of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Royal_Society "President of the Royal Society") and former Chief Executive and Director of the [Francis Crick Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Crick_Institute "Francis Crick Institute"). He was awarded the 2001 [Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physiology_or_Medicine "Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine"), along with [Leland Hartwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leland_Hartwell "Leland Hartwell") and [Tim Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Hunt "Tim Hunt"), for their discoveries of protein molecules that control the division of cells in the [cell cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_cycle "Cell cycle").

## Early life and education

Nurse's mother went from London to [Norwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich "Norwich") and lived with relatives while awaiting Paul's birth (at the age of 18) in order to hide [illegitimacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illegitimacy "Illegitimacy"). For the rest of their lives, his maternal grandmother pretended to be his mother, and his mother pretended to be his sister.

Paul was brought up by his grandparents (whom he took to be his parents) in North West London. He was educated at Lyon Park school in [Alperton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alperton "Alperton") and [Harrow County Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrow_County_School_for_Boys "Harrow County School for Boys"). He received his [BSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSc "BSc") degree in [Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology "Biology") in 1970 from the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham "University of Birmingham") and his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD "PhD") degree in 1973 from the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia "University of East Anglia") for research on *[Candida utilis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candida_utilis "Candida utilis")*. He then pursued postdoctoral work at the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern "University of Bern"), the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh "University of Edinburgh") and the [University of Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sussex "University of Sussex").

Nurse did not know that his "sister" was in fact his mother until he was in his 50s. His "parents" had both already died and his "sister" Miriam, eighteen years his senior, had died early of [multiple sclerosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_sclerosis "Multiple sclerosis"). His application for a [green card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_card "Green card") for US residency while president of [Rockefeller University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_University "Rockefeller University") was, to his surprise, rejected, despite him being a [Nobel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize "Nobel Prize") winner, president of a university and a knight; this was because he had submitted a short-form [UK birth certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_certificate#United_Kingdom "Birth certificate") which did not name his parents. When he applied for a full birth certificate he discovered the truth, to his astonishment. [Professor Turi King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turi_King "Turi King") traced his father for him in 2023.

## Career and research

Following his PhD studies, Nurse continued his [postdoctoral research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_research "Postdoctoral research") at the laboratory of [Murdoch Mitchison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murdoch_Mitchison "Murdoch Mitchison") at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh "University of Edinburgh") for the next six years (1973–1979).

Beginning in 1976, Nurse identified the gene *[cdc2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cdc2 "Cdc2")* in [fission yeast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fission_yeast "Fission yeast") (*[Schizosaccharomyces pombe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schizosaccharomyces_pombe "Schizosaccharomyces pombe")*). This gene controls the progression of the cell cycle from [G1 phase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G1_phase "G1 phase") to [S phase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S_phase "S phase"), when the cell grows in preparation for the duplication of DNA, and the transition from [G2 phase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G2_phase "G2 phase") to [mitosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitosis "Mitosis") (M), when the cell divides. In 1987, with his postdoctoral researcher [Melanie Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanie_Lee "Melanie Lee"), Nurse identified the homologous gene in human, *[Cdk1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cdk1 "Cdk1").* These genes activate and inactivate [cyclin dependent kinase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclin_dependent_kinase "Cyclin dependent kinase") (CDK) by causing phosphate groups to be added or removed.

In 1984, Nurse joined the [Imperial Cancer Research Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Cancer_Research_Fund "Imperial Cancer Research Fund") (ICRF, now [Cancer Research UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer_Research_UK "Cancer Research UK")). He left in 1988 to chair the department of microbiology at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford"). He then returned to the ICRF as Director of Research in 1993, and in 1996, was named Director General of the ICRF, which became Cancer Research UK in 2002. In 2003, he became president of [Rockefeller University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_University "Rockefeller University") in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") where he continued work on the cell cycle of fission yeast. In 2011 Nurse became the first Director and Chief Executive of the [UK Centre for Medical Research and Innovation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Centre_for_Medical_Research_and_Innovation "UK Centre for Medical Research and Innovation"), now the [Francis Crick Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Crick_Institute "Francis Crick Institute").

On 30 November 2010, Nurse succeeded astrophysicist [Martin Rees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Rees "Martin Rees") for a five-year term as [President of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Royal_Society "President of the Royal Society") until 2015.

Nurse has said good scientists must have passion "to know the answer to the questions" that interest them, along with good technical ability, and a set of attitudes including intellectual honesty, self-criticism, open-mindedness and scepticism.

## Awards and honours

In addition to the Nobel Prize, Nurse has received numerous awards and honours. He was elected an [EMBO Member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMBO_Member "EMBO Member") in 1987 and a [Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS) in 1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_1989 "List of Fellows of the Royal Society elected in 1989") and a Founder Member of the [Academy of Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Medical_Sciences "Academy of Medical Sciences") in 1998. In 1995, he was awarded the Pezcoller-AACR International Award. he received a [Royal Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Medal "Royal Medal") and became a foreign associate of the U.S. [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences "National Academy of Sciences"). He received the [Albert Lasker Award for Basic Medical Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Lasker_Award_for_Basic_Medical_Research "Albert Lasker Award for Basic Medical Research") in 1998. Nurse was [knighted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Bachelor "Knight Bachelor") in 1999. He was awarded the French [Legion d'Honneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_d'Honneur "Legion d'Honneur") and the Golden Plate Award of the [American Academy of Achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Achievement "American Academy of Achievement") in 2002. He was also awarded the [Copley Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copley_Medal "Copley Medal") in 2005. He was elected a Foreign Honorary Member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences "American Academy of Arts and Sciences") – one of the top honours – in April 2006. He is a member of the Advisory Council for the [Campaign for Science and Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campaign_for_Science_and_Engineering "Campaign for Science and Engineering"). Nurse is the 2007 recipient of the Hope Funds Award of Excellence in Basic Research. He is a Freeman of the London Borough of Harrow. In 2013, he was awarded the [Albert Einstein World Award of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Einstein_World_Award_of_Science "Albert Einstein World Award of Science") by the [World Cultural Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Cultural_Council "World Cultural Council"). In 2015, he was elected a foreign academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences "Chinese Academy of Sciences"), and won the 10th annual Henry G. Friesen International Prize in Health Research, in Ottawa, Canada. He was appointed [Member of the Order of the Companions of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Order_of_the_Companions_of_Honour "Member of the Order of the Companions of Honour") (CH) in the [2022 New Year Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_New_Year_Honours "2022 New Year Honours") for services to science and medicine in the UK and abroad. In November 2022, he was appointed to the Order of Merit.

Nurse has received more than sixty honorary degrees and fellowships, including from the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath "University of Bath") in 2002, the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford") in 2003, the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge "University of Cambridge") in 2003, the [University of Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kent "University of Kent") in 2012, the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick "University of Warwick") (Doctor of Science) the [University of Worcester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Worcester "University of Worcester") (Doctor of Science) in 2013, [City, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City,_University_of_London "City, University of London") (Doctor of Science) in 2014, [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University "McGill University") (Doctor of Science) in 2017, and the [University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hong_Kong "University of Hong Kong") (Doctor of Science) in 2021. In 2020, he was awarded an honorary degree from the [Mendel University in Brno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendel_University_in_Brno "Mendel University in Brno") in the Czech Republic.

He was also appointed an [Honorary Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_Fellow_of_the_Royal_Academy_of_Engineering "Honorary Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering") (HonFREng) in 2012 and Honorary Fellow of the British Association (HonFBA) in 2013. In July 2016, it was announced that he would become the next Chancellor of the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol "University of Bristol"). He is an Honorary Liveryman of the [Worshipful Company of Scientific Instrument Makers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worshipful_Company_of_Scientific_Instrument_Makers "Worshipful Company of Scientific Instrument Makers"). On 23 November 2024, he was elected the 169th President of [The Birmingham & Midland Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_&_Midland_Institute "Birmingham & Midland Institute") by its members. In 2023, Nurse was awarded the [Dalton Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalton_Medal "Dalton Medal") of the [Manchester Literary and Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Literary_and_Philosophical_Society "Manchester Literary and Philosophical Society").

## Personal life

Nurse married Anne Teresa (née Talbott) in 1971; they have two daughters – Sarah, who works for [ITV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITV_(TV_network) "ITV (TV network)"), and Emily, a physicist based at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London "University College London") and [CERN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CERN "CERN"). He describes himself as a sceptical agnostic.

### Political views

Nurse has been a member of the [Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_(UK) "Labour Party (UK)") for nearly 40 years and is a patron of [Scientists for Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientists_for_Labour "Scientists for Labour"), a [socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist "Socialist") society affiliated to them. In September 2020, he was a co-author on a letter in *[Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_(journal) "Nature (journal)")* alongside the former prime minister [Gordon Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Brown "Gordon Brown") highlighting the importance of [EU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU "EU") funding in the fight against [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19 "COVID-19").

As an undergraduate student at Birmingham, Nurse sold *[Socialist Worker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Worker "Socialist Worker")*, and participated in an occupation of the vice-chancellor's office. As a graduate student at East Anglia, he continued to sell *Socialist Worker*, and was sympathetic to the [International Socialist Tendency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Socialist_Tendency "International Socialist Tendency") but never formally joined the movement.

Nurse has criticised potential [Republican Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_(United_States) "Republican Party (United States)") candidates for the US presidential nomination for opposing the teaching of [natural selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_selection "Natural selection"), [stem cell research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stem_cell_research_policy "Stem cell research policy") on cell lines from human embryos, and [anthropogenic climate change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropogenic_climate_change "Anthropogenic climate change"), even partially blaming scientists for not speaking up. He was alarmed that this could happen in the US, a world leader in science, "the home of [Benjamin Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Franklin "Benjamin Franklin"), [Richard Feynman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Feynman "Richard Feynman") and [Jim Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_D._Watson "James D. Watson")".

One problem, Nurse said, was "treating scientific discussion as if it were political debate", using rhetorical tricks rather than logic. Another was the state of science teaching in the schools, which does not teach citizens how to discuss science – particularly in religious schools, even in the United Kingdom. Nurse has written that "we need to emphasise why the scientific process is such a reliable generator of knowledge with its respect for evidence, for scepticism, for consistency of approach, for the constant testing of ideas." Furthermore, Nurse feels that scientific leaders "have a responsibility to expose the bunkum". They should take on politicians, and expose nonsense during elections.

In August 2014, Nurse was one of 200 public figures who were signatories to a letter to *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* expressing their hope that Scotland would vote to remain part of the United Kingdom in September's [referendum on that issue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Scottish_independence_referendum "2014 Scottish independence referendum").

Nurse believes that scientists should speak out about science in public affairs and challenge politicians who support policies based on [pseudoscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoscience "Pseudoscience").

As the President of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society "Royal Society"), Nurse defended [Elon Musk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elon_Musk "Elon Musk")'s membership of that society.
