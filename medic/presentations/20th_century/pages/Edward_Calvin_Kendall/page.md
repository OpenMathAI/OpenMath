**Edward Calvin Kendall** (March 8, 1886 – May 4, 1972) was an American [biochemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biochemist "Biochemist"). In 1950, Kendall was awarded the [Nobel Prize for Physiology or Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_for_Physiology_or_Medicine "Nobel Prize for Physiology or Medicine") along with Swiss chemist [Tadeusz Reichstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadeusz_Reichstein "Tadeusz Reichstein") and [Mayo Clinic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_Clinic "Mayo Clinic") physician [Philip S. Hench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Showalter_Hench "Philip Showalter Hench"), for their work with the hormones of the [adrenal glands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrenal_gland "Adrenal gland"). Kendall not only researched the adrenal glands, he also isolated [thyroxine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thyroxine "Thyroxine"), a hormone of the [thyroid gland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thyroid_gland "Thyroid gland") and worked with the team that crystallized [glutathione](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glutathione "Glutathione") and identified its chemical structure.

Kendall was a biochemist at the Graduate School of the [Mayo Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_Foundation "Mayo Foundation") at the time of the Nobel award. He received his education at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University"). After retiring from his job with the Mayo Foundation, Kendall joined the faculty at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University "Princeton University"), where he remained until his death in 1972. Kendall Elementary School, in [Norwalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwalk,_Connecticut "Norwalk, Connecticut") is named for him.

## Early life and education

Kendall was born in [South Norwalk, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Norwalk,_Connecticut "South Norwalk, Connecticut") in 1886. He attended [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University"), earning a Bachelor of Science degree in 1908, a Master of Science degree in Chemistry in 1909, and a Ph.D. in Chemistry in 1910.

## Research career

After obtaining his Ph.D., his first job was in research for [Parke, Davis and Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parke,_Davis_and_Company "Parke, Davis and Company"), and his first task was to isolate the hormone associated with the [thyroid gland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thyroid_gland "Thyroid gland"). He continued this research at [St. Luke's Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Luke's-Roosevelt_Hospital_Center "St. Luke's-Roosevelt Hospital Center") in New York until 1914. He was appointed Head of the Biochemistry Section in the Graduate School of the [Mayo Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_Foundation "Mayo Foundation"), and the following year he was appointed as the Director of the Division of Biochemistry.

Kendall made several significant contributions to biochemistry and medicine. His most important discovery was the isolation of [thyroxine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thyroxine "Thyroxine"), although it was not the work for which he received the most accolades. Along with associates, Kendall was involved with the isolation of [glutathione](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glutathione "Glutathione") and determining its structure. He also isolated several steroids from the [adrenal gland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrenal_gland "Adrenal gland") cortex, one of which was initially called Compound E. Working with [Mayo Clinic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_Clinic "Mayo Clinic") physician [Philip Showalter Hench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Showalter_Hench "Philip Showalter Hench"), Compound E was used to treat [rheumatoid arthritis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rheumatoid_arthritis "Rheumatoid arthritis"). The compound was eventually named [cortisone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cortisone "Cortisone"). In 1950, Kendall and Hench, along with Swiss chemist [Tadeus Reichstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadeus_Reichstein "Tadeus Reichstein") were awarded the 1950 [Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physiology_or_Medicine "Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine") for "their discoveries relating to the hormones of the adrenal cortex, their structure and biological effects." His Nobel lecture focused on the basic research that led to his award, and was titled "The Development of Cortisone As a Therapeutic Agent." As of the 2010 awards, Kendall and Hench were the only Nobel Laureates to be affiliated with Mayo Clinic.

Kendall was elected to the United States [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences "National Academy of Sciences") in 1950, and both the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences "American Academy of Arts and Sciences") and the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society "American Philosophical Society") in 1951.

Kendall's career at Mayo ended in 1951, when he reached mandatory retirement age. He moved on to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University "Princeton University"), where he was a visiting professor in the Department of Biochemistry. He remained affiliated with Princeton until his death in 1972. In addition to the Nobel Prize, Kendall received other major awards including the [Lasker Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lasker_Award "Lasker Award"), the [Passano Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passano_Foundation "Passano Foundation") Award and the [Cameron Prize for Therapeutics of the University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Prize_for_Therapeutics_of_the_University_of_Edinburgh "Cameron Prize for Therapeutics of the University of Edinburgh"). Kendall received the Golden Plate Award of the [American Academy of Achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Achievement "Academy of Achievement") in 1966. He was awarded honorary doctorates from the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati "University of Cincinnati"), [Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Reserve_University "Western Reserve University"), [Williams College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_College "Williams College"), [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University "Yale University"), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University"), [National University of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Ireland "National University of Ireland"), and [Gustavus Adolphus College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavus_Adolphus_College "Gustavus Adolphus College").

## Family life

Kendall married Rebecca Kennedy in 1915, and they had four children. He died in 1972 in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey "Princeton, New Jersey"). His wife died in 1973.
