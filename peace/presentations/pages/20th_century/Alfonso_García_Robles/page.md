**Alfonso García Robles** (20 March 1911 – 2 September 1991) was a Mexican [diplomat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomat "Diplomat") and [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician "Politician") who, in conjunction with [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden "Sweden")'s [Alva Myrdal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alva_Myrdal "Alva Myrdal"), received the [Nobel Peace Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Peace_Prize "Nobel Peace Prize") in 1982.

García Robles was born in [Zamora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamora,_Michoacán "Zamora, Michoacán"), [Michoacán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michoacán "Michoacán"), and trained in law at the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico "National Autonomous University of Mexico") (UNAM), the [Institute of Higher International Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Higher_International_Studies "Institute of Higher International Studies") (IHEI) in Paris, France (1936), and [The Hague Academy of International Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague_Academy_of_International_Law "The Hague Academy of International Law") in the Netherlands (1938) before joining his country's foreign service in 1939.

He served as a delegate to the 1945 [San Francisco Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Conference_on_International_Organization "United Nations Conference on International Organization") that established the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations "United Nations").
He was ambassador to [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil "Brazil") from 1962 to 1964, and was state secretary to the ministry of foreign affairs from 1964 to 1970. In 1971–1975 he served as his country's representative to the United Nations before an appointment as [foreign minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_Foreign_Affairs_(Mexico) "Secretary of Foreign Affairs (Mexico)") in 1975–76. He was then appointed Mexico's permanent representative to the UN's [Committee on Disarmament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Disarmament "Committee on Disarmament").

García Robles received the Nobel Peace Prize as the driving force behind the [Treaty of Tlatelolco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Tlatelolco "Treaty of Tlatelolco"), which set up a nuclear-free zone in [Latin America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_America "Latin America") and the [Caribbean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean "Caribbean"). The agreement was signed in 1967 by most states in the region, though some states took some time to ratify the agreement.

He was admitted to the [Colegio Nacional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colegio_Nacional_(Mexico) "Colegio Nacional (Mexico)") of Mexico in 1972. His name was inscribed on the Wall of Honor of the [Palacio Legislativo de San Lázaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palacio_Legislativo_de_San_Lázaro "Palacio Legislativo de San Lázaro"), the seat of the [Chamber of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_(Mexico) "Chamber of Deputies (Mexico)"), in 2003. His widow died in 2005 aged 83.
