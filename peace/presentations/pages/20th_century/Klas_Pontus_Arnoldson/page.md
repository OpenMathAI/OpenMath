**Klas Pontus Arnoldson** (27 October 1844 – 20 February 1916) was a Swedish [author](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Author "Author"), [journalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalist "Journalist"), [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician "Politician"), and committed [pacifist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacifism "Pacifism") who received the [Nobel Peace Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Peace_Prize "Nobel Peace Prize") in 1908 with [Fredrik Bajer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredrik_Bajer "Fredrik Bajer"). He was a founding member of the [Swedish Peace and Arbitration Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Peace_and_Arbitration_Society "Swedish Peace and Arbitration Society") and a [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_parliament "Member of parliament") in the [second Chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Chamber "Second Chamber") of 1882–1887.

## Early life

Arnoldson became the railway clerk and rose to the post of stationmaster in the year 1871 to 1881. He left the railways and devoted entirely into politics. In 1881, he was elected to the riksdag, the Swedish parliament.

## Works

He attempted to shape the public opinion of both Norway and Sweden in favour of peaceful settlement. He also wrote journalistic pieces such as "Is World Peace Possible?", "Religion in the Light of Research", and "The Hope of the Centuries".
