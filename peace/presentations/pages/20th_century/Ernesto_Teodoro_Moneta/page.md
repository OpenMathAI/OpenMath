**Ernesto Teodoro Moneta** (Italian: [[eɾˈnɛsto teoˈdɔːɾo moˈneːta]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Italian "Help:IPA/Italian"); September 20, 1833 – February 10, 1918) was an Italian journalist, nationalist, revolutionary soldier and later a pacifist and Nobel Peace Prize Laureate. He adopted the motto *In varietate unitas!* which later inspired the [motto of the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motto_of_the_European_Union "Motto of the European Union").

At age 15, Moneta participated in the [Five Days of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Days_of_Milan "Five Days of Milan") (1848 uprising against Austrian rule). He later attended the military academy in [Ivrea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivrea "Ivrea"). In 1859 he joined [Garibaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garibaldi "Garibaldi")'s [Expedition of the Thousand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expedition_of_the_Thousand "Expedition of the Thousand"), and also fought in the ranks of the Italian army [against the Austrians in 1866](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Italian_War_of_Independence "Third Italian War of Independence").

Subsequently, he became an international [peace activist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_movement "Peace movement").

Between 1867 and 1896 he was editor of the Milan democratic paper *[Il Secolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il_Secolo?action=edit&redlink=1 "Il Secolo")*, published by [Edoardo Sonzogno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edoardo_Sonzogno "Edoardo Sonzogno").

In 1890 he founded the Lombard Association for Peace and Arbitration (Unione Lombarda per la Pace e l'Arbitrato), which called for disarmament and envisaged the creation of a [League of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_of_Nations "League of Nations") and [Permanent Court of Arbitration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_Court_of_Arbitration "Permanent Court of Arbitration"). He won (with [Louis Renault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Renault_(jurist) "Louis Renault (jurist)")) the [Nobel Peace Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Peace_Prize "Nobel Peace Prize") in 1907.
