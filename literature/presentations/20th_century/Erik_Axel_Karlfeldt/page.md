**Erik Axel Karlfeldt** (20 July 1864 – 8 April 1931) was a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden "Sweden") poet whose highly symbolist poetry masquerading as regionalism was popular and won him the [1931 Nobel Prize in Literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931_Nobel_Prize_in_Literature "1931 Nobel Prize in Literature") posthumously after he had been nominated by [Nathan Söderblom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Söderblom "Nathan Söderblom"), member of the [Swedish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Academy "Swedish Academy"). Karlfeldt had been offered the award already in [1919](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1919_Nobel_Prize_in_Literature "1919 Nobel Prize in Literature") but refused to accept it, because of his position as permanent secretary to the [Swedish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Academy "Swedish Academy") (1913–1931), which awards the prize.

## Biography

Karlfeldt was born into a farmer's family in [Karlbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlbo "Karlbo"), in the province of [Dalarna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalarna "Dalarna"). Initially, his name was *Erik Axel Eriksson*, but he assumed his new name in 1889, wanting to distance himself from his father, who had suffered the disgrace of a criminal conviction. He studied at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University "Uppsala University"), simultaneously supporting himself by teaching school in several places, including [Djursholms samskola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djursholms_samskola "Djursholms samskola") in the [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm "Stockholm") suburb of [Djursholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djursholm "Djursholm") and at a school for adults. After completing his studies, he held a position at the [Royal Library of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Library_of_Sweden "Royal Library of Sweden"), in Stockholm, for five years.

In 1904, Karlfeldt was elected a member of the [Swedish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Academy "Swedish Academy") and held chair number 11. In 1905, he was elected a member of the Nobel Institute of the academy, and, in 1907, of the Nobel Committee. In 1912, he was elected permanent secretary of the academy, a position he held until his death.

[Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University "Uppsala University"), Karlfeldt's alma mater, awarded him the title of [Doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy "Doctor of Philosophy") [honoris causae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honoris_causae "Honoris causae") in 1917.

Karlfeldt was a [pantheist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantheism "Pantheism").
