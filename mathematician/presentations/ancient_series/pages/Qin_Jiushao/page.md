**Qin Jiushao** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language "Chinese language"): 秦九韶; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin "Pinyin"): *Qín Jiǔsháo*; [Wade–Giles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade–Giles "Wade–Giles"): ***Ch'in Chiu-shao***, ca. 1202–1261), [courtesy name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courtesy_name "Courtesy name") **Daogu** (道古), was a Chinese mathematician, meteorologist, inventor, politician, and writer of the [Southern Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_dynasty#Southern_Song,_1127–1279 "Song dynasty"). He is credited for discovering [Horner's method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horner's_method "Horner's method") as well as inventing [Tianchi basins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianchi_basin "Tianchi basin"), a type of rain gauge instrument used to gather meteorological data.

## Biography

Although Qin Jiushao was born in [Ziyang, Sichuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziyang,_Sichuan "Ziyang, Sichuan"), his family came from [Shandong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong "Shandong") province. He is regarded as one of the greatest mathematicians in Chinese history. This is especially remarkable because Qin did not devote his life to [mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics "Mathematics"). He was accomplished in many other fields and held a series of bureaucratic positions in several [Chinese provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_provinces "Chinese provinces").

Qin wrote *Shùshū Jiǔzhāng* (*[Mathematical Treatise in Nine Sections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Treatise_in_Nine_Sections "Mathematical Treatise in Nine Sections")*) in 1247 CE. This treatise covered a variety of topics including indeterminate equations, and the numerical solution of certain polynomial equations up to the tenth [degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_of_a_polynomial "Degree of a polynomial"). The treatise addressed [surveying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveying "Surveying") and military matters. Qin included a general form of the [Chinese remainder theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_remainder_theorem "Chinese remainder theorem") that used *Da yan shu* (大衍术) or [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithm "Algorithm") to solve it. In geometry, he discovered "Qin Jiushao's formula" for finding the area of a triangle from the given lengths of three sides. This formula is the same as [Heron's formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heron's_formula "Heron's formula"), proved by [Heron of Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heron_of_Alexandria "Heron of Alexandria") about 60 BCE, though knowledge of the formula may go back to [Archimedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedes "Archimedes").

As precipitation was important agriculture and food production, Qin developed [rain gauges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_gauge "Rain gauge") that were widely used in 1247 during the [Mongol Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongol_Empire "Mongol Empire")/[Southern Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Song_dynasty "Southern Song dynasty") to gather meteorological data. Qin Jiushao later records the application of rainfall measurements in the mathematical treatise. The book also discusses the use of large [snow gauges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_gauge "Snow gauge") made from bamboo situated in mountain passes and uplands, which are speculated to be first referenced to snow measurement.

Qin recorded the earliest explanation of how [Chinese calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_calendar "Chinese calendar") experts calculated [astronomical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy "Astronomy") data according to the timing of the [winter solstice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongzhi_(solar_term) "Dongzhi (solar term)"). He also introduced the use of the [zero symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/0_(number) "0 (number)") into written [Chinese mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_mathematics "Chinese mathematics"). He developed summation of [arithmetic series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_series "Arithmetic series"), in particular the interpretation of a [polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial "Polynomial") as a nested sequence of arbitrary sums and multiples of a given number. This method of polynomial evaluation is now referred to as [Horner's method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horner's_method "Horner's method").

After he completed his work on mathematics, he ventured into politics. As a government official he was boastful, corrupt, and was accused of bribery and of poisoning his enemies. As a result, he was relieved of his duties multiple times. Yet in spite of these problems he managed to become very wealthy.
