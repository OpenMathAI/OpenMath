**Vladimir Abramovich Rokhlin** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language "Russian language"): Владимир Абрамович Рохлин; 23 August 1919 – 3 December 1984) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR "USSR") [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician "Mathematician"), who made numerous contributions in [algebraic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_topology "Algebraic topology"), [geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry "Geometry"), [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory "Measure theory"), [probability theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_theory "Probability theory"), [ergodic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergodic_theory "Ergodic theory") and [entropy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropy "Entropy").

## Life

Vladimir Abramovich Rokhlin was born in [Baku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku "Baku"), [Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_Democratic_Republic "Azerbaijan Democratic Republic"), to a wealthy Jewish family. His mother, Henrietta Emmanuilovna Levenson, had studied medicine in France (she died in Baku in 1923, believed to have been killed during civil unrest provoked by an epidemic). His maternal grandmother, Clara Levenson, had been one of the first female doctors in Russia. His maternal grandfather Emmanuil Levenson was a wealthy businessman (he was also the illegitimate father of [Korney Chukovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korney_Chukovsky "Korney Chukovsky"), who was thus Henrietta's half-brother). Vladimir Rokhlin's father Abram Veniaminovich Rokhlin was a well-known social democrat (he was imprisoned during Stalin's [Great Purge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Purge "Great Purge"), and executed in 1941).

Vladimir Rokhlin entered [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University "Moscow State University") in 1935. His advisor was [Abraham Plessner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Plessner "Abraham Plessner"). In 1940, he graduated from the [MSU Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics "MSU Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics").He volunteered for the army in 1941, leading to four years as a [prisoner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner_of_war "Prisoner of war") of a [German war camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_POW_camps_in_Germany "List of POW camps in Germany"). During this time he was able to hide his Jewish origins from the Nazis. Rokhlin was liberated by the Soviet military in January 1945. He then served as a German language translator for the 5th Army of the Belorussian front. In May 1945 he was sent to a Soviet 'verification camp' for former prisoners of war. In January 1946 he was transferred to another camp to determine if he was an "enemy of the Soviet." Rokhlin was cleared in June 1946 but was forced to remain in the camp as a guard. Due to intercession by mathematicians [Andrey Kolmogorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Kolmogorov "Andrey Kolmogorov") and [Lev Pontryagin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lev_Pontryagin "Lev Pontryagin"), he was released in December 1946 and allowed to return to [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow "Moscow"), after which he returned to mathematics. At Pontryagin's suggestion, he joined [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute_of_Mathematics "Steklov Institute of Mathematics") as Pontryagin's official research assistant.

In the 1950s, during the [anti-cosmopolitan campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-cosmopolitan_campaign "Anti-cosmopolitan campaign"), he was forced to resign from Steklov Institute of Mathematics and leave the capital. In 1952-1955 he worked in higher education institutions of [Arkhangelsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkhangelsk "Arkhangelsk"). In 1955 Rokhlin and his family managed to move closer to Moscow, to academic institutes. Since September 1955 he has been a professor of the Department of Mathematical Analysis at the [Ivanovo State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivanovo_State_University "Ivanovo State University"), since 1957 he has been a professor of the Department of Mathematics at the Kolomna Pedagogical Institute.

In 1959 Rokhlin joined [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University "Saint Petersburg State University") as a faculty member. He died in 1984 in [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad "Leningrad"). His students include [Viatcheslav Kharlamov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viatcheslav_M._Kharlamov "Viatcheslav M. Kharlamov"), [Yakov Eliashberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakov_Eliashberg "Yakov Eliashberg"), [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_(mathematician) "Mikhail Gromov (mathematician)"), [Nikolai V. Ivanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_V._Ivanov "Nikolai V. Ivanov"), [Anatoly Vershik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoly_Vershik "Anatoly Vershik") and [Oleg Viro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleg_Viro "Oleg Viro").

## Work

Rokhlin's contributions to [topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology "Topology") include [Rokhlin's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rokhlin's_theorem "Rokhlin's theorem"), a result of 1952 on the [signature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signature_(topology) "Signature (topology)") of [4-manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-manifold "4-manifold"). He also worked in the theory of [characteristic classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_class "Characteristic class"), [homotopy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_theory "Homotopy theory"), [cobordism theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobordism_theory "Cobordism theory"), and in the topology of [real algebraic varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_algebraic_geometry "Real algebraic geometry").

In [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory "Measure theory"), Rokhlin introduced what are now called [Rokhlin partitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rokhlin_partition?action=edit&redlink=1 "Rokhlin partition"). He introduced the notion of [standard probability space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_probability_space "Standard probability space"), and characterised such spaces up to [isomorphism mod 0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_probability_space#Isomorphism_modulo_zero "Standard probability space"). He also [proved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_proof "Mathematical proof") the famous [Rokhlin lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rokhlin_lemma "Rokhlin lemma").

## Family

His son [Vladimir Rokhlin, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Rokhlin,_Jr. "Vladimir Rokhlin, Jr.") is a well-known mathematician and computer scientist at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University "Yale University").

Rokhlin's uncle was [Korney Chukovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korney_Chukovsky "Korney Chukovsky"), a well-known Russian poet, most famous for his popular children's books.
