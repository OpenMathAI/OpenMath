**Giovanni Bianchini** (in [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin "Latin"), **Johannes Blanchinus**) (1410 – c. 1469) was a professor of [mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics "Mathematics") and [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy "Astronomy") at the [University of Ferrara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ferrara "University of Ferrara") and court astrologer of [Leonello d'Este](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonello_d'Este "Leonello d'Este"). He was an associate of [Georg Purbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Purbach "Georg Purbach") and [Regiomontanus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regiomontanus "Regiomontanus"). The letters exchanged with Regiomontanus in 1463–1464 mention works by Bianchini entitled: *Primum mobile* ([astronomical tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomical_table "Astronomical table") included), *Flores almagesti*, *Compositio instrumenti*.

Bianchini was the first mathematician in Europe to use decimal [positional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positional_notation "Positional notation") [fractions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraction_(mathematics) "Fraction (mathematics)") for his [trigonometric tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonometric_table "Trigonometric table"), at the same time as [Al-Kashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Kashi "Al-Kashi") in [Samarkand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarkand "Samarkand"). He was also probably the first to use the [decimal point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decimal_separator "Decimal separator"). In *De arithmetica*, part of the *Flores almagesti*, he uses operations with negative numbers and expresses the *Law of Signs*.

He was probably the father of the instrument maker Antonio Bianchino.

The crater [Blanchinus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blanchinus_(crater) "Blanchinus (crater)") on the [Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon "Moon") is named after him.

## See also

Giovanni Bianchini should not be confused with two similarly-named Italians with their own lunar craters: [Francesco Bianchini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Bianchini "Francesco Bianchini") (1662–1729) (and the [Bianchini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bianchini_(crater) "Bianchini (crater)") crater), and [Giuseppe Biancani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Biancani "Giuseppe Biancani") (1566–1624) (and the [Blancanus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blancanus_(crater) "Blancanus (crater)") crater).
