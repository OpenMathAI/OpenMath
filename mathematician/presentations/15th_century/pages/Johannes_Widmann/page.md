**Johannes Widmann** (c. 1460 – after 1498) was a German [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician "Mathematician"). The [+ and - symbols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plus_and_minus_signs "Plus and minus signs") first appeared in print in his book *Mercantile Arithmetic* or *Behende und hüpsche Rechenung auff allen Kauffmanschafft* published in Leipzig in 1489 in reference to surpluses and deficits in business problems.

Born in [Eger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheb "Cheb"), [Bohemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemia "Bohemia"), Widmann attended the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig "University of Leipzig") in the 1480s. In 1482 he earned his "[Baccalaureus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts "Bachelor of Arts")" (Bachelor of Art degree) and in 1485 his "[Magister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magister_degree "Magister degree")" (doctorate).

Widman published *Behende und hübsche Rechenung auff allen Kauffmanschafft* ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language "German language"); i.e. Nimble and neat calculation in all trades), his work making use of the signs, in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig "Leipzig") in 1489. Further editions were published in [Pforzheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pforzheim "Pforzheim"), [Hagenau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagenau "Hagenau"), and [Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augsburg "Augsburg").
Handwritten entries in a surviving collection show that after earning his "Magister" Widman announced holding lectures on e.g. calculating on the lines of a calculating board and on algebra. There is evidence that the lecture on algebra actually took place, making it the first known university lecture on this topic.

Around 1495 Widmann published the Latin writings *Algorithmus integrorum cum probis annexis*, *Algorithmus linealis*, *Algorithmus minutiarum phisicarum*, *Algorithmus minutiarum vulgarium*, *Regula falsi apud philosophantes augmenti et decrementi appellata und Tractatus proportionum plusquam aureus*.

He died in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig "Leipzig").

When [Adam Ries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Ries "Adam Ries") was in [Erfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erfurt "Erfurt") between 1518 and 1522 he got to know Widmann's algebra lecture script (today in the Saxon State Library) wherefrom he took examples for his own writings.
