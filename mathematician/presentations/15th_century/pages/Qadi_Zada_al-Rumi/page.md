Qāḍī Zāda al-Rūmī

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9f/Kazi_Zade_Rumi_Mausoleum_01.jpg/250px-Kazi_Zade_Rumi_Mausoleum_01.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Kazi_Zade_Rumi_Mausoleum_01.jpg)

Qāḍī Zāda al-Rūmī Mausoleum in [Shah-i-Zinda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah-i-Zinda "Shah-i-Zinda"), [Samarkand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarkand "Samarkand")

***Qāḍī Zāda al-Rūmī*** (1364 in [Bursa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bursa "Bursa"), [Ottoman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Empire "Ottoman Empire") – 1436 in [Samarqand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarqand "Samarqand"), [Timurid Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timurid_Empire "Timurid Empire")), whose actual name was **Salah al-Din Musa Pasha** (**qāḍī zāda** means "son of the judge", *al-rūmī* "the Roman" indicating he came from [Asia Minor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Minor "Asia Minor"), which was once [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire "Roman Empire")), was a [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people "Turkish people") [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer "Astronomer") and [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician "Mathematician") who worked at the [observatory in Samarkand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulugh_Beg_Observatory "Ulugh Beg Observatory"). He computed sin 1° to an accuracy of 10−12.

Together with [Ulugh Beg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulugh_Beg "Ulugh Beg"), [al-Kāshī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Kāshī "Al-Kāshī") and a few other astronomers, Qāḍī Zāda produced the *[Zij-i-Sultani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zij-i-Sultani "Zij-i-Sultani")*, the first comprehensive stellar catalogue since the [Maragheh observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maragheh_observatory "Maragheh observatory")'s *[Zij-i Ilkhani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zij-i_Ilkhani "Zij-i Ilkhani")* two centuries earlier. The *Zij-i Sultani* contained the positions of 992 stars.

## His works

- *Sharh al-Mulakhkhas* (Commentary on Jaghmini's compendium on the science of Astronomy)
- *Sharh Ashkal al-Ta'sis* (Commentary on Samarkandi's Arithmetics)
