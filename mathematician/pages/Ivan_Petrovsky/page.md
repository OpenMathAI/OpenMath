**Ivan Georgiyevich Petrovsky** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language "Russian language"): Иван Георгиевич Петровский; 18 January 1901 – 15 January 1973) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union "Soviet Union") [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician "Mathematician") working mainly in the field of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equation "Partial differential equation"). He greatly contributed to the solution of [Hilbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Hilbert "David Hilbert")'s [19th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_nineteenth_problem "Hilbert's nineteenth problem") and [16th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_sixteenth_problem "Hilbert's sixteenth problem") problems, and discovered what are now called [Petrovsky lacunas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrovsky_lacuna "Petrovsky lacuna"). He also worked on the theories of [boundary value problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_value_problem "Boundary value problem"), [probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability "Probability"), and on the [topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology "Topology") of [algebraic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_curve "Algebraic curve") and [surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_surface "Algebraic surface").

## Biography

Petrovsky was a student of [Dmitri Egorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Egorov "Dmitri Egorov"). Among his students were [Olga Ladyzhenskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Ladyzhenskaya "Olga Ladyzhenskaya"), [Evgenii Landis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgenii_Landis "Evgenii Landis"), [Olga Oleinik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Oleinik "Olga Oleinik") and [Sergei Godunov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Godunov "Sergei Godunov").

Petrovsky taught at [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute_of_Mathematics "Steklov Institute of Mathematics"). He was a member of the [Academy of Sciences of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_Soviet_Union "Academy of Sciences of the Soviet Union") since 1946 and was awarded [Hero of Socialist Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_Socialist_Labour "Hero of Socialist Labour") in 1969. He was the president of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University "Moscow State University") (1951–1973) and the head of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Mathematicians "International Congress of Mathematicians") ([Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow "Moscow"), 1966).

He died in Moscow, and was buried in the [Novodevichy Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novodevichy_Cemetery "Novodevichy Cemetery").
