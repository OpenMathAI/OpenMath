**Andrey Nikolayevich Tikhonov** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language "Russian language"): Андре́й Никола́евич Ти́хонов; 17 October 1906 – 7 October 1993) was a leading [Soviet Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR "USSR") [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician "Mathematician") and [geophysicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysicist "Geophysicist") known for important contributions to [topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology "Topology"), [functional analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_analysis "Functional analysis"), [mathematical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physics "Mathematical physics"), and [ill-posed problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ill-posed_problem "Ill-posed problem"). He was also one of the inventors of the [magnetotellurics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetotellurics "Magnetotellurics") method in geophysics. Other transliterations of his surname include "Tychonoff", "Tychonov", "Tihonov", "Tichonov".

## Biography

Born in [Gzhatsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gzhatsk "Gzhatsk"), he studied at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University "Moscow State University") where he received a Ph.D. in 1927 under the direction of [Pavel Sergeevich Alexandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Sergeevich_Alexandrov "Pavel Sergeevich Alexandrov"). In 1933 he was appointed as a professor at Moscow State University. He became a corresponding member of the [USSR Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_Academy_of_Sciences "USSR Academy of Sciences") on 29 January 1939 and a [full member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academician "Academician") of the USSR Academy of Sciences on 1 July 1966.

## Research work

Tikhonov worked in a number of different fields in mathematics. He made important contributions to [topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology "Topology"), [functional analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_analysis "Functional analysis"), [mathematical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physics "Mathematical physics"), and certain classes of [ill-posed problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ill-posed_problem "Ill-posed problem"). [Tikhonov regularization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tikhonov_regularization "Tikhonov regularization"), one of the most widely used methods to solve ill-posed [inverse problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_problem "Inverse problem"), is named in his honor. He is best known for his work on topology, including the [metrization theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrization_theorems "Metrization theorems") he proved in 1926, and the [Tychonoff's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tychonoff's_theorem "Tychonoff's theorem"), which states that every product of arbitrarily many [compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_space "Compact space") [topological spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_space "Topological space") is again [compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_space "Compact space"). In his honor, [completely regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completely_regular "Completely regular") topological spaces are also named *[Tychonoff spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tychonoff_space "Tychonoff space")*.

In mathematical physics, he proved the fundamental [uniqueness theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniqueness_theorem "Uniqueness theorem") for the [heat equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_equation "Heat equation") and studied [Volterra integral equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volterra_integral_equation "Volterra integral equation").

He founded the theory of [asymptotic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_analysis "Asymptotic analysis") for differential equations with small parameter in the leading derivative.

## Organizer work

Tikhonov played the leading role in founding the [Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Computational_Mathematics_and_Cybernetics "MSU Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics") of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University "Moscow State University") and served as its first dean during the period of 1970–1990.

## Awards

Tikhonov received numerous honors and awards for his work, including the [Lenin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Prize "Lenin Prize") (1966) and the [Hero of Socialist Labor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_Socialist_Labor "Hero of Socialist Labor") (1954, 1986).
