Tadashi Nakayama (mathematician)

**Tadashi Nakayama** (中山 正, *Nakayama Tadashi*; July 26, 1912, [Tokyo Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Prefecture "Tokyo Prefecture") – June 5, 1964, [Nagoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya "Nagoya")) was a [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician "Mathematician") who made important contributions to [representation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representation_theory "Representation theory").

## Career

He received his degrees from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo "University of Tokyo") and the [University of Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Osaka "University of Osaka") and held permanent positions at the University of Osaka and [Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University "Nagoya University"). He had visiting positions at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University "Princeton University"), [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign "University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign"), and the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg "University of Hamburg"). [Nakayama's lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakayama's_lemma "Nakayama's lemma"), [Nakayama algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakayama_algebra "Nakayama algebra"), [Nakayama's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakayama's_conjecture "Nakayama's conjecture"), and the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murnaghan–Nakayama_rule "Murnaghan–Nakayama rule") are named after him.
