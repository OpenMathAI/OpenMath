**Erik Ivar Fredholm** (7 April 1866 – 17 August 1927) was a Swedish [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician "Mathematician") whose work on [integral equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_equation "Integral equation") and [operator theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_theory "Operator theory") foreshadowed the theory of [Hilbert spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_space "Hilbert space").

## Biography

Fredholm was born in Stockholm in 1866. He obtained his PhD at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University "Uppsala University") in 1898, under the supervision of [Gösta Mittag-Leffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gösta_Mittag-Leffler "Gösta Mittag-Leffler"). He was *docent* at [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University "Stockholm University") from 1898 to 1906 and professor from 1906 until his death. In 1914 he was elected a member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences "Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences") and in 1922 a foreign member of the [Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters "Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters"). Beside his academic career he was recruited to the [Swedish Social Insurance Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Social_Insurance_Agency "Swedish Social Insurance Agency") when it was founded in 1902. He later served as an actuary at the insurance company Skandia (1904-1927), where his Fredholm equation was used to calculate buyback-prices.

From 1911, he was married to Agnes Maria Liljeblad, the secretary of [Mittag-Leffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mittag-Leffler "Mittag-Leffler").

## Mathematical work

In (Fredholm [1900](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Ivar_Fredholm#CITEREFFredholm1900), [1903](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Ivar_Fredholm#CITEREFFredholm1903)), Fredholm introduced and analysed a class of integral equations now called [Fredholm equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_equation "Fredholm equation"). His [analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_theory "Fredholm theory") included the construction of [Fredholm determinants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_determinant "Fredholm determinant"), and the proof of the [Fredholm theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_theorem "Fredholm theorem").

## Honours

Fredholm was a member of the [Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters "Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters") and of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei "Accademia dei Lincei"); he was awarded the [Poncelet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poncelet_Prize "Poncelet Prize") for 1908.

The lunar crater *[Fredholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_(crater) "Fredholm (crater)")* is named after him as is the asteroid [21659 Fredholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21659_Fredholm "21659 Fredholm").
