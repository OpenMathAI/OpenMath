Zhao Shuang (simplified Chinese: 赵爽; traditional Chinese: 趙爽; pinyin: Zhào Shuǎng; born 21 June 1990[1]) is a basketball player for China women's national basketball team. She is part of the squad for the 2012 Summer Olympics.[2]
